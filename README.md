## What is Readspace?

Readspace is a privacy-first reading platform that brings together your favorite sources—RSS feeds, saved articles, Twitter threads, Reddit posts, and newsletters—into one calm, intelligent inbox.

No ads. No clutter. No subscriptions required to do basic things.

It’s built for people who actually want to read what they save.


## What's wrong with the tools we have?

**Feedly** pivoted toward enterprise threat intelligence. The core reading experience has stagnated—slow, bloated, and full of upsells.

**Readwise Reader** is still in beta years later. It's buggy, inconsistent, and locked behind a $100/year paywall. It's proprietary, closed, and frustrating to use at scale.

Both lock your reading into their systems. Neither respects the basics: speed, clarity, reliability.


## Reviving what worked

Google Reader made RSS powerful, fast, and beautiful. It was killed off in 2013, and nothing has fully replaced it since.

We're bringing it back—with a modern interface, broader content support, local-first sync, and a reading experience that feels like home.


## A better reading inbox

- Fast, minimal, and responsive UI
- Real offline and local-first support
- Syncs across devices
- Works with RSS, newsletters, saved links, Reddit, Twitter, and more
- No tracking or third-party analytics


## AI that actually helps

Optional AI integrations, done right:

- Filter out low-quality or repetitive content
- Discover new feeds based on what you already read
- Smart search and semantic querying
- Automatically tag and organize articles
- Summarize dense or longform content

These features can run locally using open models. You control where and how they’re used.
